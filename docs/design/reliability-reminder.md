# Reliability Reminder

## Decision

End-to-end reliability is one fixed, release-owned advisory with
repository-owned scope. It addresses robustness theater: defensive machinery
that looks locally rigorous while increasing total complexity or system-wide
failure risk. Configuration selects modules and exact source paths but cannot
supply arbitrary reminder text. This keeps the operating principle
version-matched, reviewable, and consistent across AI harnesses without turning
repository data into an open prompt surface.

The policy model validates selectors. The repository's existing candidate and
direct-module facts resolve matches. The engine constructs one structured
reminder for both task packets and reports, while the CLI owns its bounded human
rendering. No second module or path-matching algorithm belongs in the renderer.

## Surfaces

`task-start` evaluates its planned selection. A match adds the reminder to the
complete packet, adds the policy document to workflow documents, and schedules
one read-only next action before implementation. The ordinary design-context
payload continues to carry repository-specific rationale.

Checkpoint and merge gates evaluate their actual candidate paths. Normal gate
reports include the reminder for matching candidates, including passed,
finding-bearing, and already-passed results. Operational errors retain the
existing minimal gate and review headers rather than rendering advisory
reminders. The reminder is part of the normal report model and pinned report
schema, not gate-run acceptance evidence.

## Advisory boundary

The reminder contributes no finding, status, receipt, identity binding,
authorization, review selection, or command. It remains absent when no selector
matches. Its principle and four questions are fixed in source and repeated in
the public policy document. Repository-specific reliability and recovery
requirements stay in mapped design documents and executable boundary tests.
